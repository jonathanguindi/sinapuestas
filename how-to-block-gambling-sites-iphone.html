Option 1: with the SinApuestas app (the most complete)

  1. Download SinApuestas from the App Store. It's free.
  2. Open the app and choose your country, so it blocks the most-used betting sites there first.
  3. Tap “Block Gambling” and choose how long: 7, 30 or 90 days, 1 year, or forever.
  4. Allow the permissions your iPhone asks for. From then on, betting sites won't load.
  5. Optional: add a trusted person you can call with one tap when an urge hits.

The app blocks more than 1,700 sportsbooks, online casinos, crypto casinos and prediction markets worldwide, and the list updates itself. While the commitment lasts, the block can't be removed and the app can't be deleted.

Option 2: with Screen Time (free, one site at a time)

  1. Open Settings › Screen Time and turn on Content & Privacy Restrictions.
  2. Go to Store, Web, Siri & Game Center Content › Web Content and choose Limit Adult Websites.
  3. Under Never Allow, add the address of each betting site you used.
  4. Ask someone else to set the Screen Time passcode and keep it to themselves.

Menu names may vary slightly depending on your iOS version.

If gambling is hurting you

Blocking sites helps, but it's support, not treatment. Talk to someone you trust or a professional. Gamblers Anonymous has free groups worldwide. In the US you can call or text 1-800-GAMBLER; in the UK, the National Gambling Helpline is 0808 8020 133.
